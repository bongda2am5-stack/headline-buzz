Gordon Ramsay has a new title, and it might be his favourite one yet: grandpa. The celebrity chef's daughter Holly Ramsay-Peaty gave birth to a baby girl on Wednesday, November 25, and the family announced the happy news themselves on Instagram on Thanksgiving Day, November 26. The baby's name is Daphne June Ramsay-Peaty, and she is the first grandchild for Gordon and his wife Tana.

![Newborn baby's tiny feet resting on a soft blanket](/images/gordon-ramsay-grandpa-baby-daphne-01.jpg)

*Photo: Oleskandra Biliak / Pexels*

Holly, 26, shared the news in an Instagram post on Thursday, writing: "Daphne June Ramsay-Peaty, Welcome to the World. You'll forever be our beautiful baby girl and George's baby sister. Thank you to all the Doctors, Nurses and Midwives who made sure we were safe, healthy and happy — we will forever be grateful."

The post included sweet photos from the hospital, showing the new parents holding their newborn daughter. Within hours, the announcement had become one of the most talked-about celebrity stories of the Thanksgiving holiday — and Gordon's own reaction may have been the best part of all.

## Gordon's first message to his granddaughter

Gordon, 60, did not wait long to welcome his granddaughter publicly. Commenting on Holly's post, he wrote: "Welcome Miss Daphne, congratulations to Adam & Holly wow wow wow."

He then shared the news on his own Instagram account, thanking Holly and her husband for "the perfect early Christmas present" and signing off with "#grandpa & grandma Finally". For a man famous on television for shouting in kitchens, the message was pure softness — and fans loved it.

It was a fitting end to a remarkable week for the chef. Just days earlier, at his Hollywood Walk of Fame ceremony, Ramsay had told the crowd that the family was about to grow. "We welcome our first grandchild in a couple of days from now," he said during his acceptance speech, adding: "The Ramsay household is getting bigger."

![A newborn baby's feet cradled gently in a mother's hands](/images/gordon-ramsay-grandpa-baby-daphne-02.jpg)

*Photo: Valeria Boltneva / Pexels*

Ramsay has also already picked out his grandfather name. He told PEOPLE after the Walk of Fame ceremony that he plans to go by "Papa G" — "Papa G. with a star in Hollywood!" he said. It is a nickname that suits a man who has spent more than 25 years on television: warm, a little bit showy, and impossible to forget.

## Who are Holly and Adam?

![A newborn baby holding a parent's finger](/images/gordon-ramsay-grandpa-baby-daphne-03.jpg)

*Photo: SERHAT TUĞ / Pexels*

Holly Ramsay-Peaty is one of Gordon and Tana Ramsay's six children; the couple have been married since 1996.

Holly married Olympic swimmer Adam Peaty on December 27, 2025, at Bath Abbey in England, after two years together. Peaty, 31, is one of Britain's most decorated swimmers, a multiple Olympic gold medallist, and Daphne is the couple's first child together. Adam is already a father to six-year-old George, his son from a previous relationship — which is why Holly's announcement called Daphne "George's baby sister". The little boy now has a baby sister to look out for, and the Ramsay-Peaty household has grown by one very small, very loved person.

The couple announced in June that they were expecting a baby girl, writing at the time: "Baby Ramsay-Peaty coming December 2026. We can't wait to meet our baby girl." Daphne arrived a little ahead of that December schedule, just in time for the family's first Christmas as grandparents and parents of a newborn.

## Why this announcement matters

![Parents' hands touching their baby's hand](/images/gordon-ramsay-grandpa-baby-daphne-04.jpg)

*Photo: juliane Monari / Pexels*

Celebrity baby news often arrives through a magazine cover or a representative's statement. This one was different: the family told the story themselves, in their own words, on their own accounts. Holly posted the hospital photos and the name. Gordon posted his own message of thanks. There was no mystery and no rumour mill — just a family sharing good news on a holiday built around gratitude. Everything in this story — the name, the date, the quotes, the nickname — comes from the family's own posts and from interviews they gave themselves.

The timing helped, too. The announcement landed on Thanksgiving Day in the United States, when millions of people are scrolling through their phones between dinner and dessert. Within a day, the story had been picked up by PEOPLE, Page Six, Parade and Just Jared, all citing the family's Instagram posts.

## A big week for the Ramsay family

![Christmas gift boxes with red ribbons](/images/gordon-ramsay-grandpa-baby-daphne-05.jpg)

*Photo: Laura James / Pexels*

For Gordon Ramsay, the arrival of Daphne caps off a remarkable stretch. The chef, who turned 60 this year, received his star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame in the television category, recognising a career that has taken him from Michelin-starred kitchens to some of the biggest food shows on television, including Hell's Kitchen, Kitchen Nightmares and MasterChef.

What comes next for the family is the part no camera will capture: the quiet weeks at home, the first Christmas with a newborn in the house, and a little girl growing up with a grandfather who already has a nickname picked out. "Papa G" has a nice ring to it.
