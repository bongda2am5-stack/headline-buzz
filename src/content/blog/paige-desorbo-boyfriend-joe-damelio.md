Paige DeSorbo has finally let the world in. The Summer House alum, 34, shared a video featuring her boyfriend, sports marketing executive Joe D'Amelio, on social media for the first time on Wednesday, November 25 — the night before Thanksgiving — ending nearly two years of keeping their relationship almost entirely off camera.

The video, posted to TikTok and shared to her Instagram Stories, shows DeSorbo and her loved ones making homemade pizzas together. Set to a voiceover from Martin Scorsese's 1990 film Goodfellas — the scene where Ray Liotta's Henry Hill introduces his crew — the camera pans across the group as each person is introduced. D'Amelio appears in the clip lifting his drink in greeting as the "Nickey Eyes" line plays.

DeSorbo captioned the video simply: "We aren't Italian at all."

Fans spotted the milestone immediately. "It's TikTok official okayyy honey," one commenter wrote, while another added that D'Amelio "fits in so well" with her family. For a star who has spent the past two years guarding this relationship, putting her boyfriend in the frame was the loudest statement she could make without saying a word.

## A hard launch, Paige-style

The word fans keep using is "hard launch" — the moment a celebrity stops hinting and finally shows their partner outright. DeSorbo's version was characteristically low-key and funny: no posed couple photo, no gushing caption, just her boyfriend folded into a chaotic, joyful pizza night with the people she loves most.

That fits everything she has said about this relationship. Where her previous romance played out in public for three years, this one has been deliberately quiet — she has spoken about D'Amelio only in interviews and on her Giggly Squad podcast, calling it "really fun" and "very relaxing."

Within a day, the clip had been picked up by PEOPLE, E! News, Us Weekly and Just Jared — all noting the same thing: this was the first time D'Amelio had appeared on her social media at all.

## Who is Joe D'Amelio?

Joe D'Amelio, 37, is a sports marketing executive — senior vice president of football marketing at Klutch Sports Group, the agency founded by Rich Paul that represents some of the biggest names in sports.

The two were first spotted together at a Philadelphia Eagles game in January 2025, sparking the first romance rumours. Us Weekly confirmed that July that DeSorbo had moved on from her previous relationship and was "quietly dating" D'Amelio. In February 2026, she confirmed to Us Weekly that the relationship was serious. "Yes, we're exclusive," she said at the time. A month later, she told PEOPLE she was off the market: "I'm seeing someone and I have been for a little bit."

The couple kept their private life together almost entirely off social media until now — no couple photos, no tagged posts, no shared holidays on the grid. The pizza video changes that, and fans are reading it as a sign that DeSorbo is finally comfortable letting this relationship exist in public.

## The engagement rumour she shut down

The hard launch comes about four months after DeSorbo had to deny rumours that she and D'Amelio had already gotten engaged.

In July 2026, during a family vacation in Italy, DeSorbo posted a photo of herself wearing a white dress. Fans quickly decided the couple must have gotten engaged on the trip, and congratulatory messages poured in. On the July 17 episode of Giggly Squad, the podcast she co-hosts with Hannah Berner, DeSorbo set the record straight — with a joke.

"Can I wear a white dress and have a laugh?" she said, making it very clear that she is not engaged.

The real engagement on that trip belonged to someone else in the family: her brother, Gary DeSorbo, proposed to his girlfriend during the vacation — which is where much of the confusion came from.

The mix-up was a reminder of how closely fans watch her life — and perhaps why she waited so long to share D'Amelio on her own accounts. By introducing him herself, on her own terms, in a video about pizza night with her crew, DeSorbo took the story back.

## A new chapter after Craig Conover

The relationship with D'Amelio is DeSorbo's first since her split from Southern Charm star Craig Conover. The two dated for three years before announcing their breakup in December 2024.

Since then, DeSorbo has been open about wanting something different: calmer, more private, and on her own schedule. The pizza video is the clearest picture yet of what that looks like: a night in with friends and family, and her boyfriend right there in the middle of it.

There was no magazine cover, no red-carpet debut, no representative's statement. Just a TikTok, a Goodfellas voiceover, and a caption about not being Italian. The fans did the rest.

## Why this one matters

Celebrity "hard launches" have become their own kind of event — the moment a star decides a relationship is real enough to show. For DeSorbo, whose last relationship was documented almost in real time on reality television, keeping this one private for nearly two years, then revealing it in such a casual, joyful way, says a lot about where she is now.

Everything in this story comes from DeSorbo herself or from interviews she gave: her own TikTok and Instagram Stories, her own words to Us Weekly and PEOPLE, and her own podcast. There is no anonymous source and no rumour — just a woman who decided, on the night before Thanksgiving, that it was time to let her boyfriend into the frame. After two years of keeping it quiet, Paige DeSorbo is done hiding her happiness — and her fans could not be happier for her.
